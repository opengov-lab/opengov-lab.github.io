# Kickoff ICAI OpenGov Lab at LAB42

Yesterday, the ICAI OpenGov Lab had its official kick-off at LAB42 UvA at Science Park in Amsterdam! 
The lab's Dual PhD candidates Damiaan Reijnaers, Lisa Winters, and Patricia Moeskops presented their research plans to the lab and partners from Rijksorganisatie voor Informatiehuishouding (RvIHH) and I-Partnerschap!

*Lisa Winters, Erna Ruijer, Jaap Kamps, Paul van den Akker, Marion Hermans-Koelemij, Peter van der Donk, Patricia Panhuis, Patricia Moeskops, Damiaan Reijnaers, Sander Verhoeckx, and David Graus in front of LAB42*

It was great to dive into the substance and initial research plans around information management, AI, archival studies and disinformation. 
We're looking forward to seeing the first results from the lab's students. Stay tuned to stay in the loop!

Thank you for attending, sharing your perspectives, and enthusiasm: Erna Ruijer, Jaap Kamps, Paul van den Akker, Marion Hermans-Koelemij, Peter van der Donk, Patricia Panhuis, Sander Verhoeckx, David Graus, Sue Wade (📸), and Maarten Marx (not pictured, teaching duties)!
