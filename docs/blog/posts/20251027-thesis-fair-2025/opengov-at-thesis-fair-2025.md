# OpenGov Lab at the UvA Thesis Fair 2025

On Friday, October 17th, a delegation (David, Jaap, Damiaan & Maik) of the ICAI OpenGov Lab was present at the UvA (AI) Thesis Fair to talk to prospective thesis students!

![David & Maik in action, accompanied by our newly designed OpenGov banners!](thesis-fair.jpg?w=768){ loading=lazy }
*David & Maik in action, accompanied by our newly designed OpenGov banners!*

<!-- more -->

Surrounded by the marble walls, pillars and busts of the beautiful Royal Tropical Institute (KIT) in Amsterdam, we hope to have inspired students from a broad (for our field) selection of Master programmes (AI, Data Science, Information Systems, Computational Science, Computer Science) to come work with us on exciting projects. As we have just started, we are full of ideas but not of time! We are still open to students that are interested in one of our projects (listed on the bottom of this page), so don't hesitate to [contact](/contact) us!
