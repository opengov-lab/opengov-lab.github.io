# Floris Bos Wins Short Paper Award with OpenGov Thesis

Congratulations to Floris Bos (not pictured), whose Computer Science MSc thesis *"Linking References to Documents in Parliamentary Debates"* won the Best Short Paper Award at [The 29th International Conference on Theory and Practice of Digital Libraries (TPDL2025)](https://tpdl2025.github.io/) in Tampere, Finland! 

![Maarten Marx (pictured left) receives the Best Short Paper Award at TPDL2025 on Floris Bos' behalf](tpdl-award.jpg?w=768){ loading=lazy }
*Maarten Marx (left) with the Best Short Paper Award for Floris' paper at TPDL2025*

<!-- more -->

His paper, based on his thesis was co-authored with Marc van Opijnen (Logius, [KOOP](https://www.koopoverheid.nl/)) and [Maarten Marx](https://www.opengov.nl/team/#maarten-marx) (who received the award in Tampere on Floris' behalf) and was carried out at the ICAI OpenGov Lab.
