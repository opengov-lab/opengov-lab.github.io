On September 28th, David Graus and Maik Larooij present the session **"AI en de Woo: voorbij de hype"** at the [Dag van de Woo-professional](https://www.acoi.nl/kennisdeling/dag-van-de-woo-professional/). AI is often seen as *the* solution to the challenges of Woo requests, but is it? Together with participants, we explore what AI can and cannot do in the context of the Dutch Open Government Act (Woo). Drawing on current international research and concrete examples from practice, the session gives Woo professionals the grounding to engage critically and constructively in the conversation about using AI in their organisation.

📍 Location: Spoorwegmuseum, Maliebaanstation 16, Utrecht  
🕐 Time: 14:20–15:30
